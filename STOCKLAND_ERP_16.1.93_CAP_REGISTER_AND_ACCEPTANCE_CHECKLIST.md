## Status rules

- **OPEN / BLOCKER:** must pass before release or live go-live.
- **OPEN / OWNER ACTION:** requires an authorized Stockland HR, Finance, Owner, or site operator; do not infer or auto-create business data.
- **SOURCE FIX — HOST CHECK OPEN:** code-level patch or local regression exists, but target-host acceptance is still required.
- **PASS (LIMITED):** the named static/local test passed only; it does not prove live-site operation.
- **CLOSED:** only permitted when reproducible evidence, reviewer, build identity, and acceptance result are attached to the same release candidate.

## Payroll-law source and sign-off checklist

The code currently declares a versioned 2025 PAYE rule and local regression fixtures. Public sources support the revised monthly bands of ETB 0–2,000 at 0%, 2,001–4,000 at 15%, 4,001–7,000 at 20%, 7,001–10,000 at 25%, 10,001–14,000 at 30%, and over 14,000 at 35%; the tax rules source must be linked to its effective date in the installed configuration. The employee/employer pension contribution rates are reported as 7%/11% under the private-organization pension scheme. These sources confirm the broad headline rates, **not** all allowance interpretations, individual exemptions, or Stockland’s particular employee records.

- [Federal Negarit Gazette No. 64 (1 September 2025), Income Tax Amendment Proclamation 1395/2025](https://www.ffic.gov.et/Portals/0/1395-2017.pdf)
- [Ministry of Finance — tax directives index](https://www.mofed.gov.et/mof-directive/tax-directive/)
- [Ministry of Justice — Pension Contribution Directive No. 1087/2025](https://justice.gov.et/en/directives/)
- [PwC Ethiopia individual income-tax summary, last reviewed 15 July 2026](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/ethiopia/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)
- [Institute for Fiscal Studies, Survey of the Ethiopian Tax System 2025–26](https://ifs.org.uk/publications/survey-ethiopian-tax-system-2025-26)

**Required legal conclusion:** a local payroll/tax professional must confirm the currently effective primary law, implementation date, allowance/exemption limits and all related directives before the Owner signs the rule matrix. Do not use an older secondary guide’s allowance limits as law without confirming the current directive. This checklist is an engineering/control CAP, not a legal opinion.

## Release-gate order

1. Resolve/record missing employee compensation through HR/Owner, and obtain statutory rule-matrix sign-off.
2. Build the candidate from the exact latest branch head; pass every exact-head CI/security workflow and inspect the actual ZIP.
3. Pass deterministic source/ledger/GL/report/import/authorization regression suites with independent expected values.
4. Back up and restore a staging copy; run authorized deep System Health and all role-separated SOPs; document each FAIL/UNKNOWN.
5. Verify the actual hosting environment, desktop theme, mobile PWA, every dropdown/button/report handler, print/CSV output and cross-location denials.
6. Obtain the Owner’s go/no-go, perform the controlled update, verify installed version/hash and run post-deployment smoke/deep checks; preserve rollback materials.

**No-go conditions:** stale or unbuilt artifact; any failed exact-head critical workflow; unresolved required compensation classification; unexplained report/ledger/GL mismatch; permission or location leakage; any unbound consequential control; failed restore/rollback; or any deep-health FAIL/UNKNOWN without documented resolution.
